README Update

•  How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this? 

	User stories represent plain and transparent desires of the users that need to be fulfilled in the application of the software product under consideration. User stories are viewed as requirement for creating designs of an iterative by dissecting each story onto simple executable ideas that could be applied to software coding. As the stories continue to stream in, and the requirements continue to take complete and plausible outlook, reviews and retrospectives of iterative add the fine details of valuable products. 


•  How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work? 

	Developing programs take a life of their own, starting from vague and incomplete requirements that could initiate few sprints of Agile scrums. The adaptive process then seeks relevant details by probing the user’s needs and wishes, through cyclical iterations. In such cooperative and collaborative interactions between development team and end users, the final vision of the program development becomes clear to both sides, creators and users. 

	Because the Agile framework is founded on expedience of valuable product, timely and critical communications require transparency and discipline in order to align the effort of the development team with the needs of the end users. The premise of engaging in continuous exploration and experimentation of both sides, creators and consumers, enforces the fact that both sides are striving to define the boundaries of productive and valuable contractual process of adaptive and flexible product delivery.  

•  What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

	Being a good team member implies accountability for helping the team to accomplish its ultimate goal of delivering valuable and expedient product. That requires punctuality and transparency in communication and participation in the various events of Agile Scrums, empowered by the critical tools of communication of feedback, responses, and inquiries. Because an Agile scrum team represents an intellectual group of thinkers, engaging in explorative creation of adaptive iterative, the team member plays high-stakes in forming and applying ideas that result in successful deliverable of cyclical products.
